Proprietary Tools

Six Instruments. One Strategic System.

Each tool delivers standalone analytical value while contributing to the broader platform intelligence. Access any tool directly or engage the full advisory ecosystem.

Tool 01

Paycheck Protection Score

The Paycheck Protection Score directly measures the financial burden a health plan places on individual employees by benchmarking premium contributions against actual take-home pay. This tool makes the invisible visible — converting abstract plan costs into a clear metric of employee financial health.

Open Tool →
What It Does
  • Calculates the percentage of net income consumed by employee health plan contributions
  • Identifies employee cohorts at elevated financial risk due to premium burden
  • Benchmarks contribution levels against workforce compensation bands
  • Generates a scored output for plan design and affordability review
Why It Matters

When health plan contributions consume a disproportionate share of employee wages, employers face downstream consequences: voluntary turnover, reduced productivity, and talent acquisition failure. The Paycheck Protection Score gives plan sponsors an objective, defensible metric to evaluate employee-facing plan cost — and the evidence needed to act on it.

Tool 02

Fiduciary Guard

Fiduciary Guard is a risk assessment and documentation framework designed for employers with self-funded or partially self-funded health plans. It evaluates plan governance against ERISA fiduciary standards and identifies areas of legal and financial exposure at the executive and board level.

Open Tool →
What It Does
  • Assesses plan governance against ERISA fiduciary obligations
  • Identifies documentation gaps that create personal liability exposure
  • Evaluates vendor oversight, claims review, and plan amendment processes
  • Produces a defensible risk profile and remediation priority list
Why It Matters

ERISA fiduciary liability is personal. Board members, executives, and named plan administrators can face individual legal and financial consequences when plan governance fails. Fiduciary Guard provides the structured oversight framework that separates organizations with documented governance from those operating on assumption.

Tool 03

Actuarial Value Calculator

The Actuarial Value (AV) Calculator quantifies the relative generosity of a health plan design by measuring the percentage of total allowed costs the plan pays on behalf of a standard population. This metric is central to ACA compliance, benefit benchmarking, and plan design strategy.

Open Tool →
What It Does
  • Calculates plan-level actuarial value based on cost-sharing design inputs
  • Benchmarks plan design against ACA metal tier thresholds
  • Models the employee cost-sharing impact of design changes
  • Supports benefit comparability analysis for multi-plan or multi-location employers
Why It Matters

Actuarial value is the foundational measure of what a health plan is actually worth to the employee who uses it. Without this calculation, benefit design decisions are made without a calibrated reference point — creating misaligned cost structures, compliance gaps, and compensation equity issues across the workforce.

Tool 04

Trend Calculator

The Trend Calculator applies credible medical cost trend data to an employer's current health plan spend to produce multi-year cost projections. It gives CFOs and HR leadership a forward-looking model to stress-test funding assumptions and inform budget strategy before renewal.

Open Tool →
What It Does
  • Projects future plan cost using configurable medical and pharmacy trend rates
  • Models one-, three-, and five-year cost trajectories by input scenario
  • Generates employer and employee cost-share projections under each scenario
  • Provides benchmark context against national health cost trend indices
Why It Matters

Health plan trend is the primary driver of long-term cost exposure for employers — yet most organizations enter renewal without a credible forward model. The Trend Calculator converts reactive budgeting into proactive financial governance, giving plan sponsors the data architecture needed to make informed, defensible funding decisions.

Tool 05

Affordability Calculator

The Affordability Calculator determines whether an employer's lowest-cost health plan option meets ACA affordability thresholds for each employee cohort. It provides the compliance infrastructure necessary to defend against employer shared responsibility penalties and workforce equity concerns.

Open Tool →
What It Does
  • Calculates employee premium contributions as a percentage of household income under all three ACA safe harbors
  • Flags employees for whom the current plan fails to meet affordability standards
  • Models premium adjustment scenarios to restore affordability compliance
  • Generates a contribution-by-cohort compliance summary for audit readiness
Why It Matters

ACA employer shared responsibility penalties are calculated per employee and accumulate rapidly when affordability standards are not met. Beyond compliance, affordability directly determines whether lower-wage employees can access the plan — a workforce stability issue that carries financial and reputational consequences for the organization.

Tool 06

Employee Giveback Calculator

The Employee Giveback Calculator transforms health plan savings into a concrete reinvestment strategy. When employers reduce health plan cost through smarter plan design, the tool models exactly how those savings can be redirected into wages, retirement contributions, enhanced benefits, and workforce development — making health plan efficiency a cultural and competitive asset.

Open Tool →
What It Does
  • Quantifies total employer savings generated by plan design changes or cost-reduction strategies
  • Models reallocation scenarios across wages, 401(k) match, PTO, and supplemental benefits
  • Calculates per-employee reinvestment value at multiple workforce size tiers
  • Produces a shareable reinvestment summary for board reporting and employee communications
Why It Matters

Health plan cost reduction is not simply a finance outcome — it is a workforce investment decision. The Employee Giveback Calculator makes that connection explicit and quantifiable, enabling employers to communicate the direct link between plan efficiency and employee compensation. This positions health plan strategy as a driver of organizational culture and long-term workforce alignment.
